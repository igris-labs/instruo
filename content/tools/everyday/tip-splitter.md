## Split fairly

Enter the bill before tip, pick a percentage, and choose a whole number of people. The breakdown shows the bill, tip, total, and each person's share. When the smallest currency unit cannot divide evenly, the result explains how many people pay one unit extra.

Currency selection controls formatting and rounding; it does not convert exchange rates. JPY uses whole yen, while the other supported currencies use two fractional digits. Copy the breakdown to share it with your group.
