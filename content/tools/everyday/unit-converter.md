## Convert a measurement

Choose a category, enter an amount, and pick source and target units. Results and equivalent measurements update immediately. Swap reverses the units while keeping your input amount.

Volume uses US customary cups, fluid ounces, and gallons. Digital storage distinguishes decimal KB/MB/GB from binary KiB/MiB/GiB. Negative temperatures are allowed down to absolute zero. Results are rounded to at most eight decimal places.
